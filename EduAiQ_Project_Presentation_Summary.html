EduAiQ Technical Deep-Dive
Modules & Security Suite
100% DRM-Grade Content Security Architecture
Zero-Piracy Student Media Protection Suite

EduAiQ employs an enterprise-grade, multi-layer security barrier protecting proprietary videos, PDF notes, and reading materials from unauthorized capture, copying, or leakage.

Anti-PrintScreen & Snipping
Intercepts PrtScn key, Win+Shift+S, Cmd+Shift, instantly blacking out the screen and wiping the clipboard with security warnings.
Loss-of-Focus Auto-Blur
When third-party screen recorders or snipping overlays are opened, the player activates a 40px heavy blur to prevent capture.
Dynamic Floating Watermark
Authenticated student username & timestamp continuously drift across the video/PDF, deterring phone camera recordings.
Global Anti-Copy & Inspect
Right-click context menu, text drag/selection, DevTools shortcuts (F12, Ctrl+Shift+I), View Source (Ctrl+U) completely blocked.
Secure Canvas PDF Engine
PDFs are converted to isolated HTML5 canvas frames with disabled downloads, preventing raw PDF URL theft.
Dual-Source Fallback Stream
Direct MP4/WebM video streaming with strict cross-origin policies, eliminating YouTube Error 153 & embed syndication bans.
