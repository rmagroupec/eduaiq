Syllabus Breakdown

Click a subject to see the topic scope for each level. All topics listed are examinable — nothing beyond this scope is asked.

Math Olympiad Syllabus
Primary (Grades 3-5): Number sense, basic arithmetic, simple fractions, shapes and measurement, pattern recognition.

Middle (Grades 6-8): Ratios and proportions, integers, basic algebra, geometry fundamentals, data handling and averages.

Senior (Grades 9-10): Advanced algebra, coordinate geometry, trigonometry basics, probability, and multi-step word problems.
Science Olympiad Syllabus
Primary (Grades 3-5): Living and non-living things, plants and animals, basic states of matter, the five senses, simple machines.

Middle (Grades 6-8): Cell structure, motion and force, acids and bases, the human body, ecosystems.

Senior (Grades 9-10): Chemical reactions, laws of motion, genetics fundamentals, electricity and magnetism, environmental science.
AI Olympiad Syllabus
Primary (Grades 3-5): What is AI, everyday examples of AI, simple logic and pattern puzzles.

Middle (Grades 6-8): How machines "learn," basic algorithmic thinking, introduction to data and predictions.

Senior (Grades 9-10): Machine learning concepts, classification vs. prediction, real-world AI ethics scenarios, and beginner logic used in the AI Lab.
