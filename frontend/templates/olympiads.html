Olympiads

Compete. Get Measured. Get Recognized.

EduAiQ Olympiads are structured competitions in Math, Science, and AI, built to measure a student's real understanding — not just memorization. Each Olympiad follows a fixed curriculum so students and schools know exactly what to prepare for.

Every participant receives a performance report, and top performers earn certificates, medals, and scholarships toward EduAiQ's AI Lab and Skill Development programs — turning one exam into an ongoing learning path.
